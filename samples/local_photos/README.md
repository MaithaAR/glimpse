# Phone photos (OOD / robustness set)

14 real phone photos by the author (EXIF stripped, resized to <=1280 px): home-cooked meals, cafe
and restaurant dishes, desserts and snacks. Used for the out-of-distribution evaluation -- how the
unfamiliar-photo check scores real phone photos vs the CGMacros study photos -- and as spares for
the instructor set. Not training data.
